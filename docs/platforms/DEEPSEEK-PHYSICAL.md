## Released responsibilities

| Topic | Role | Identity | Model policy |

DeepSeek is the dedicated adversarial platform in this release. Each challenger starts from a fresh session. Before receiving the proposer forecast, it records an independent closest-work search, effect/headroom estimate and falsification plan. It then challenges duplication, combination-only novelty, theoretical saturation, engineering demand, metric alignment, baseline fairness, resource feasibility and likely experimental failure.

An objection is not a verdict. Every material objection needs a primary source and exact locator where applicable, plus assumptions, impact and a test that could refute the challenger. Challenges go to the assigned verifier through committed files; no private message or shared memory may carry scientific content to Codex or Claude.

The historical DeepSeek onboarding packets remain evidence only. New packets must bind the challenger, assigned topic, full post-merge commit and private outbox. DeepSeek proposer and verifier identities are not released in this division of work.
