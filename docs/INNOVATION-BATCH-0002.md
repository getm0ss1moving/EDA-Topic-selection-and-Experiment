## Shared discovery requirement

Apply docs/INNOVATION-STRATEGY.md. In the first proposer release compare at least
one concrete candidate from each lens below, then choose one bounded anchored
mechanism for adversarial refinement:

1. Cooperating algorithmic agents with an explicit coordination/state mechanism.
2. Unified prediction of a bounded set of physically coupled quantities or stages.
3. Prediction with specialists, analytical/incremental methods or scoped exact work.

A short shortlist is research, not three accepted topics or three separate live
teams. Record the closest existing systems and why each candidate is pursued or
not pursued. A candidate may bridge lenses; do not add components merely to tick
all three boxes. Include strong conventional methods. Prefer a plausible causal
test and a smallest meaningful experiment over an all-of-EDA architecture.

Quality combinations are eligible, including systems contributions with known
components. Structural novelty is encouraged where justified, not compulsory.
Do not require super-additive improvements or universal new theorems. Conversely,
extra agents, features, training labels or trials are not an unexplained advantage.
Identify the proposed system-level interaction, guarantee, useful capability or
cost/reliability tradeoff and test against a faithful strongest composition.

Select a narrow stage boundary or coupled decision and keep final routability,
timing/PPA and total iteration cost relevant. Potential search questions include
how agents exchange decision dependencies, resolve conflicting changes, choose

## D05 focus

Own prediction/analysis fidelity and timing/extraction/signoff decisions. Explore
bounded joint prediction (for example coupled timing/parasitic/physical signals),
specialist selection or learning-guided exact/incremental work. Identify the
engineering decision, information available at that point, uncertainty/failure
contract and useful downstream endpoint before designing a model.

Distinguish approximate numerical accuracy, probabilistic coverage and exact
results relative to a declared model/engine. Generic predict-then-check, confidence
gating and adaptive multi-fidelity scheduling have prior art. Full-state rounded
arithmetic, context compatibility, fallback costs and production coverage cannot
be omitted when exactness is claimed. A restricted capability may be useful, but
justify its real use and report unsupported contexts rather than silently
expanding a guarantee.

## Progress and final deliverables

Each substantive revision should resolve the cheapest important uncertainty:
closest-method mapping, a lawful positive/negative witness, an operation/cost
ledger, a correctness obligation, a deployment-input audit or a minimal executable
experimental dependency plan. Treat theory, prototype hypotheses and measured
results separately. Never repeat a known documentary blocker for several rounds
without a concrete repair or a justified parking position.

The challenger independently broadens the source/contest/code search, checks
useful combinations fairly and proposes decisive falsification controls. The
verifier adjudicates exact current releases and every literal objection ID with
the compact evidence table. A component's prior existence is not a sufficient
objection; a valid same-assumption reduction or missing demonstrated delta can be.

Final English dossiers include an engineering use case, nearest-work map,
mechanism and invariants, decisive counter-hypotheses, updated task list with
producer/consumer artifacts, realistic resource requests, stop rules and honest
forecasts (null where uncalibrated). The coordinator's Chinese report summarizes
the actual verdicts, strongest surviving opportunities and unresolved issues;
it is not an additional scientific vote or a promise of acceptance.
