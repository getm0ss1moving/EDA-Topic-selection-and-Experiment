# Engineering gap and deployment audit

## User and decision

The proposed user is an implementation engineer making local datapath placement changes after CTS/initial GR. The output must be a physically legal, function-preserving movement plus repair bundle whose final extracted timing/routability justifies all iteration costs. This is an assumed deployment persona, not a completed practitioner study.

General motivation has direct evidence in S12 (publisher abstract/introduction) and S15 (§1 and physical constraints). Candidate-specific frequency, absolute time savings and acceptable integration cost are unknown. EXP-002 must resolve them before confirmation. The packet's thresholds are explicitly provisional.

## Existing open capabilities

S05/S06 show that current OpenROAD already has setup/hold repair, setup protection, shared-load handling and rollback. S07 documents incremental global routing. The proposal cannot claim those capabilities are absent. Reading these interfaces does not prove a correct end-to-end integration or license-compatible representative dataset. Current-source inspection and historical available executable are different revisions; EXP-003 resolves that mismatch.

S04's official AgenticPD repository includes an optional hold-buffer diagnostic in its knowledge graph. That is a declared heuristic, not a verified physical diagnosis on every design. The paper and code use stage-level search; no claim is made that a text search proves the absence of all action-specific planning. An earlier, non-author-linked repository was also inspected during discovery, then superseded for baseline definition by the author-linked repository. Their code and evaluation protocols must not be conflated.

## Commercial capability and negative evidence

S20's official product page advertises routing-driven placement and concurrent clock/data optimization. S14 is a concrete public disclosure of reserving future hold-fixing resources. These defeat broad claims that industry ignores downstream repair or that reservation itself is new. Commercial internal solvers, proprietary workload incidence and actual comparative performance remain unknown. Neither an open-source omission nor marketing text establishes an industry-wide algorithmic gap.

## What remains a research question

The only surviving candidate gap is whether *action-conditioned, shared and mutually competing concrete repair options* are a useful interface for selecting bounded placement edits under a tight total budget. S13 already couples small gate movement with buffer-hole planning, and S22 already couples scarce resources; the proposed difference must be compared under matching assumptions, not inferred from vocabulary.

Potential integration costs include candidate and option extraction, complete min/max constraint handling, library-safe buffer insertion, legalizer interaction, invalidation after physical changes, full rerouting checks, netlist equivalence and operator-visible failure accounting. If existing serial or monolithic lookahead reaches the same final frontier at equal cost, there is no supported new capability. A different clock/logic optimization objective would be a different topic.

## Deployment position

No industrial dataset, foundry-qualified signoff flow, representative timing-closure incidence or production access has been established. A research PDK demonstration could establish only a scoped academic result. Final DR/extraction, hold safety and all-case cost remain mandatory; a pre-route score alone is not adoption evidence. EXP-001–006 block experiment readiness while allowing continued paper research.
