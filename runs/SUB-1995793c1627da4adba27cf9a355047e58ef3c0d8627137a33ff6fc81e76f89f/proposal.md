## Retained conditional exactness/accounting contract

CLM-B4-001 (elementary conditional lemma): Let a complete deterministic obligation record q contain a slot h plus its full context. A slot identifies the rule/version, target/source identity and geometry, layer/net labels and query definition. There is at most one complete record per slot per candidate state. Slot equality is only a projection; it does **not** establish record equality. A complete-context record includes the complete spatial-query result, including the empty result, query bounds, relevant derived data and all global semantic configuration. Hashes index payloads; equality compares canonical bytes. Any marker result includes stable source identities and all output data needed by the oracle.

Let Q0 be all supported records at the common checkpoint and Q_i all supported records at sibling i. In the complete warm regime K0=Q0, define R_i=Q_i minus K0. Construct an influence-support superset E_i of candidate slots whose complete record could differ from Q0 or be newly introduced. For finite-radius rules, E_i includes every new/changed anchor and every current anchor whose complete closed query intersects **either old or new** changed geometry. Connectivity relabeling, transforms, technology/rule changes, derived geometry and global options either propagate into E_i through audited dependencies or force E_i to all slots/UNKNOWN. Object membership returned by the old query is insufficient: insertion into an empty range is an invalidator.

For any shared residual record q in R_i intersect R_j, its slot is in E_i intersect E_j. Proof: if its slot were outside E_i, the complete unchanged record would be in Q0 and hence K0, contradicting residual membership; similarly for j. Therefore disjoint supports imply zero residual sharing. With one record per slot per state and nonnegative per-slot cost upper bounds u_h, weighted shared residual work is at most U_ij=sum over h in E_i intersect E_j of u_h. Slot splitting must retain the one-record invariant or multiply by an audited multiplicity bound. This is a necessary-condition screen and work-accounting lemma, not novel general optimization mathematics, a physical incidence measurement, or a wall-time bound.

A complete warm cache is a **conditional regime**, not a free byproduct of a marker report. Cold K0=empty invalidates the influence-only screen: unchanged records may be shared. A partial cache requires adding every potentially uncovered baseline slot to each appropriate E_i; when coverage cannot be proven, use all slots/UNKNOWN. Lower overhead cannot be obtained by silently pretending the baseline certified clean obligations. The deployed adapter must price the coverage ledger and any additional enumeration.

## Concrete acquisition mechanism and repair

The mechanism remains baseline-relative complete-context observation shared across competing singleton/pair decisions, with discovery cost included. A slot query materializes its full context in declared candidate states, including empty ranges; it returns canonical payloads, exact-output/provenance masks, elapsed/CPU/bytes, and candidate/config hashes. Slot equality never certifies payload equality. Context extraction in different candidate states is charged even if predicates later deduplicate. Unknown/global/derived dependencies force full supported-domain influence or whole-reference fallback. Selected candidates finish every needed exact check, marker removal/regeneration, connectivity guard and final reference/extraction/timing work.

CLM-R2-002 (conditional decision-model finding): The old one-step stopping rule is not sufficient in complementary-observation regimes. Replace it with a bounded depth-two conditional query planner, retaining the one-step version only as an ablation. Let belief b be a finite weighted ensemble over complete candidate-cost/success/incidence tables. Let T(b,t) be the common singleton/pair terminal chooser's expected utility among choices that leave a conservative exact/fallback reserve before deadline t. Define V0=T. For d=1,2, Vd=max(T, max_a[-cost(a)+E_o V(d-1)(b|o,t-cost(a))]). This is ordinary finite-horizon value of information (R2-S06), not claimed algorithmic novelty. Conditional plans may abandon the second query after the first observation. They do not certify the predicted success of an unchecked candidate.

Research specification: at most16 candidates,128 shortlisted slot-group actions per epoch,16 finite-ensemble scenarios, depth2, at most65,536 posterior-node evaluations per epoch, at most4 acquisition epochs per pool, then choose/stop. Candidate/state ordering and ties are canonical; STOP wins ties, then ascending action ID. Each slot-group returns observations over at most16 candidate states. Shortlisting is deterministic by valid residual-work upper bound then slot ID, with UNKNOWN ranked first; if an audited bound does not exist use UNKNOWN, not a fabricated zero. Generally, savings from duplicate predicate work Wdup cannot establish positive elapsed gain when extra extraction/administration/planning/fallback exceeds Wdup; wall-time transfer also needs a scheduling model. This counterexample separates paid context discovery from predicate reuse, a missing attribution check rather than a new caching theorem.

H-R2: among independently selected physical pools with nonzero residual work after identical K0, slot-conditioned prediction can lower total observation+checking+fallback cost at the same complete-outcome quality compared to C and the mapped AiDRC arm. The strongest null is that an ordinary same-information factorized controller explains all benefit. Required factorial: representation (raster/factorized/slot) × planning (myopic/depth2) × semantic observation coupling (real/association-destroyed), keeping kernels/actions/cost accounting equal. If only lookahead helps, the slot representation has no demonstrated contribution. If shared queries save predicate work but total cost rises, the engineering hypothesis fails in that regime. If the representation only improves a proxy without checked closure/timing, utility fails. No super-additive gain or mathematical novelty is required for a useful systems result, but a benchmark win over eager acquisition alone is inadequate.
