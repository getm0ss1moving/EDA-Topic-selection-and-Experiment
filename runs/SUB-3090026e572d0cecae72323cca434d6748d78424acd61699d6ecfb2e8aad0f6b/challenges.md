## OBJ-1101 — full checking of rejected candidates needs an endpoint-matched baseline

Target: current proposal, Acquisition mechanism paragraph beginning “Every selected candidate”; tasklist EXP-104/105 and experiment-plan Data/correctness. Severity: **major fixable experimental-comparator question**, conditional on a first-clean-result endpoint. It blocks my exact-task agreement pending resolution, not the hypothesis or source research. Confidence: high in the logical distinction, unresolved in physical cost and user applicability.

Evidence: the current specification requires every selected candidate to complete all supported checks and unsupported reference work. The primary endpoint includes time to first complete connected clean target; only the returned target necessarily needs a complete clean certificate. Own C2-A01 Boolean fixture in search-log.md distinguishes a sound rejection witness from an incomplete acceptance: with a fixed candidate order and identical final reference, early rejection returns the same fully checked successful candidate while avoiding further predicates on already invalid alternatives. This is elementary conjunction logic, not an OpenROAD feature claim or a physical speedup. C2-S05 selected raw849–875 separates query traversal from per-pair predicates but does not establish interruptibility or overhead. C2-S11 is adjacent author-described physical-flow early pruning; its proxy-based placement pruning is NOT evidence of sound exact rejection in DRC.

Reasoning/applicability: for immutable siblings and an endpoint that requires zero violations, one independently sound disqualifying witness can establish that a sibling is not eligible. Completing its remaining report is additional information acquisition, potentially valuable for sharing and fallback ranking, but not logically required for rejecting that sibling. A full-check-only conventional comparator can overstate the economic opportunity addressed by sharing. This concern is not that F should lose: all arms must receive the same stop/continue actions, and F may rationally continue a rejected sibling to acquire reusable information. Charge that continuation and its opportunity benefit.

Uncertainty/limits: same-net edits can require global rule closure; transient or later-deleted markers are not sound rejection witnesses. A primitive marker only qualifies after required canonicalization/semantic checks establish it survives the named reference. Full best-effort ranking may require complete reports even for nonclean candidates. The current engine may not support safe interruption without large setup or reconstruction costs. The toy omits all these costs and cache interactions. No claim that existing baselines actually exhibit the toy savings is made.

Reversal condition: show that the frozen engineering contract requires complete reports for every selected/rejected alternative, with evidence of that demand; or prove the allowed rule subset admits no cheaper sound rejection interface; or measure that equal-action early rejection has no material benefit once reference, cache opportunity and restart costs are included. Any of these can remove or narrow this concern. Mere equal deprivation of stop actions across all arms does not establish an endpoint-matched strong baseline.

Cheapest decisive task: first source-only specify a rejection-certificate contract and distinguish CLEAN, REJECTED_BY_WITNESS, INCOMPLETE/UNKNOWN and COMPLETE_NONCLEAN_REPORT. Inspect the pinned predicates and marker lifetime. Then, only after allocation, cross C/F with full-report and witness-stop/continue modes on the same fixed pools, order, cache, deadline and final oracle. Include all-clean, early-witness, late-witness, no-clean and shared-work-lost cases. Falsify the concern if no eligible witness or material difference survives. Always finish the returned candidate's entire oracle and guards; do not credit partial output as success. Preserve the full-report mode for best-effort metrics and adapter differential tests.
