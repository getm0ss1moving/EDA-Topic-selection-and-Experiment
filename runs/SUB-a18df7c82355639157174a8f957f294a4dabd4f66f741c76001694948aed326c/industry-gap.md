# R2 engineering gap and adoption boundary

Historical undo-based candidate selection remains supported by S01, but the claimed modern integration is narrowed after valid OBJ-002. Pinned sizing generation S22 builds alternatives; S23 scores them with a local prepared delay estimator. S24/S25 provide a measured VT alternative/undo/rank/commit flow that consumes driver arrival. Neither requires the anchored complete per-candidate endpoint vector. proposal.md CLM-610 supplies the producer/query/undo/commit trace and limits. Batch sizes, repeated full contexts, fixed-wire/ideal-clock coverage and full-state restoration remain unmeasured. A prospective full-vector caller must justify its actual decision and smaller-query alternatives before this becomes a deployment contribution.

Known sharing is stronger than the R1 baseline: S20/S21 conditional execution plus corrected S05 canonical timing cache constructs the same mask scheduler (CLM-609). Sparse base state, full equality, caching, bitsets, batching, rollback and source adaptation alone are not a remaining demonstrated systems delta. No prototype advancement is justified until EXP-000R supplies a required capability or cost/reliability witness beyond faithful composition. Combining known components remains eligible; no super-additive gain is demanded.

S18-P HeteroSTA preprint §III adds explicit flattened circuit/parasitic interfaces and several report granularities to the map; S26 IncreGPUSTA adds incremental graph-update/levelization mechanisms. Their numerical semantics and hardware must not be silently mixed into an OpenSTA byte-exact comparison. No current binary, commercial engine or GPU implementation was tested. Public vendor S17 remains limited to advertising/access-restricted leads; S04/S05 patents establish disclosures, not present deployment or absence. There is no claimed commercial feature deficit, foundry-signoff equivalence or measured speedup.

Matched future comparison includes the same base/candidates, legal and output contract, libraries/wire loads/constraints, scalar kernels/FP ordering, cache/bitset/order/memory and fallback, real caller sequence and guard. Full vectors can be the offline oracle; research-only validation and deployment-essential work are separately itemized, with both in the resource ledger. A real full-vector consumer is required for the frozen deployment endpoint. No invented report expansion can establish benefit. Unsupported MCMM/hold/SI/CPPR/topology contexts require explicit scalar/full-context handling; the current restricted model does not meet ISPD27's full requirements.

Useful engineering is still possible in principle, but current evidence supports holding implementation and conditional parking, not acceptance. Resurrection requires both a same-anchor consumer and an actual B++ delta, then read-set/compatible-build/rights/owner/resource and consent gates. A driver-query, VT, prediction or other different research direction requires a fresh topic rather than rewriting EDA-0006.
