## OBJ-1201 — Prove that repartition repairs a real group-dependent constraint, not just a chosen enumeration window

**Target:** CLM-1208; proposal “Inputs, representation and output,” “Proposed causal mechanism,” and EXP-1202/1203. **Severity:** major fixable causal-identification concern before experiment confirmation; not a refutation of the stated conditional hypothesis.

**Evidence and applicability:** The released proposal fixes identical buffer master and buffer count, fixes standard-cell placement, and restricts locations through a finite displacement window. Its analytical fixture in search-log.md explicitly invents the “regrouped” domains instead of deriving them from sinks. C12-S01 raw87 supports broad displacement relevance, not these domains. C12-S04 section 3.1 has electrical/geometric clustering constraints, illustrating why sink-dependent admissibility may be meaningful. Own C12-A2 in search-log.md isolates the issue with exact runnable code and output.

**Reasoning:** Conditional lemma: if every leaf uses the same fixed master, every group has the same legal location domain P, the buffer count k is fixed, and geometry is the only feasibility predicate, then geometry feasibility is simply whether k mutually disjoint footprints can be selected from P. Relabeling or repartitioning sinks cannot change that existence statement. Group-dependent electrical/path-length limits or justified displacement contracts can break this symmetry, as can genuinely group-dependent domains. These are therefore the causal bridge to specify. If domains differ only because a narrow centroid-window generator omits otherwise acceptable locations, an apparent regrouping success may also be obtained by expanding the original grouping's domain. The proposal correctly supplies domain expansion and avoids global-UNSAT claims; it has not yet separated these explanations in its decisive task.

**Uncertainty:** This is not a theorem that real regrouping is useless: electrical objectives, upstream effects and route quality can depend on membership even with the same geometry feasibility. Real timing-constrained windows may be valuable. A2 is an invented one-site model without timing, not a CTS failure, incidence rate or gain measurement. A1's empty-domain limitation is retained and corrected openly by A2.

**Reversal:** Derive group-dependent option validity from the locked engineering contract and show that the material failed cases persist against equal-cost domain expansion/reassignment for the original grouping. If only objective costs change, restate the mechanism as cost-quality optimization without claiming a feasibility repair.

**Cheapest decisive task:** C12-T1 below, first a source/spec-only derivation, then finite tests; later allocated census compares fixed-group domain expansion, joint reassignment and core-driven exchange at identical buffer count, option budget and electrical thresholds. Include common-domain, roomy and no-contention controls. Record domain-exclusion reasons per option.
