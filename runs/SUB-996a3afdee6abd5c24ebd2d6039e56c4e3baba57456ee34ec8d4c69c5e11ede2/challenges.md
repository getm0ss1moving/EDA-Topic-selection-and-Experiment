## Nonblocking task refinement C-T201: rank error is not decision error

Target: EXP-003/005 diagnostics and CLM-009/020. A tiny own analytic witness has common matrix C=[[0,-100],[-100,1000]] and contrast D=diag(100,-0.01). Its best rank-one Frobenius approximation is Dhat=diag(100,0), with relative error approximately 0.0001. Actual best order is BA at pair (2,2), utility 1000.01. An AB-first deterministic tie in the approximation picks AB there, actual utility 999.99 and regret 0.02. These arbitrary utility units are not ns, physical measurements or proof that a chip realizes the matrices. The witness merely proves that a small global rank residual can hide a decision-relevant low-energy contrast. Rescaling changes any numerical regret threshold. The proposal already says rank screens do not prove downstream utility, so this is a test refinement, not a contradiction or new fatal objection.

Add margin-stratified order flips/regret and ties to the planned rank diagnostic. On the same complete grid, record singular residual, decision margin, chosen order and policy regret under the fixed scalarization/guardrails; do not acquire extra labels just for this check. Falsify the relevance of this concern if the locked policy and physical outcomes show low-energy modes never change a meaningful decision. High rank alone is also not proof of uselessness: apply the declared noise/economic criterion rather than rejecting every nonzero singular value. Missing-grid outcomes remain missing and require the existing fallback analysis.
