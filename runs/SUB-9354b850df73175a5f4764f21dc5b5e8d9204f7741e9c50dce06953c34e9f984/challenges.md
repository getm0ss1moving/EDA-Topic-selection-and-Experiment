## OBJ-901 — Measure sharing remaining after the common baseline, not raw overlap

**Target:** CLM-007/010; proposal “Mechanism and formal contract”; EXP-002/004/006. **Severity:** major, fixable evidence/experimental-design gap; not a refutation. **Evidence:** the released proposal starts from a complete baseline report but does not pin initial cache contents or explicitly separate baseline-common overlap from new sibling-common overlap. C-S01 §V-E and C-S02 §IV-C motivate giving the conventional comparator its legitimate incremental/reuse state. Own derivation and tiny set test are reproduced in search-log.md.

Let K0 contain all reusable supported obligations populated by checking the common checkpoint, where such certificate extraction is feasible and charged. Write R_i=Q_i minus K0. For two candidates, incremental work saved by sharing versus separate evaluations from the SAME K0 is exactly sum of w(q) over R_i intersect R_j. Raw Q_i intersect Q_j is not that saving. For example Q1={b0,b1,b2,a}, Q2={b0,b1,b2,b}, K0={b0,b1,b2}: three raw common obligations but zero new sibling saving. If R_i are pairwise disjoint, residual work is modular and this proposed overlap mechanism has no checking-work advantage, although other scheduling effects may remain.

This matters particularly because distinct repairs change their complete local contexts, while most identical untouched contexts may already be known at the common checkpoint. That last statement is an engineering hypothesis, not a measured prevalence claim. Conversely two distinct candidates can share the same newly edited local fragment while differing elsewhere; R_i intersection R_j may then be nonempty. The positive toy has one shared-new obligation and saves one of four residual units. Hence this is not a no-reuse theorem.

**Uncertainty/confidence:** high confidence in the identity; physical frequency, granularity, extraction cost and affordability of K0 unknown. A baseline marker report alone is not a cache of all clean obligations; do not pretend those certificates are free. Cold and warm regimes must both be charged honestly.

**Cheapest decisive test/reversal:** before training, source-audit whether K0 certificates can be emitted during the baseline check. After allocation, fix the same K0 in all arms, log weighted baseline-common, novel-common and candidate-unique work separately, and report constructor/lookup/invalidation/fallback cost. Reverse the practical concern if representative held-out candidates have sufficient novel-common residual savings after those costs to exceed a preregistered engineering threshold. Do not change candidate generation to manufacture overlap. This adds specificity to the existing same-cache control rather than alleging that the proposal already reported an unfair experiment.

The proposal already acknowledges identity controls, but has not frozen a distinct representation, estimator interaction or acquisition operation that makes the scientific candidate differ. **Confidence/uncertainty:** high confidence in the fixed-set reduction and conditional identity. No claim that Iyer/Bilmes implemented this EDA system, that the entire physical problem is solved, or that the strongest conventional composition necessarily wins.

**Cheapest decisive test/reversal:** give B3 and B6 the same finite score arrays and cache state and require exact selections/stop decisions to agree; no EDA execution needed.
