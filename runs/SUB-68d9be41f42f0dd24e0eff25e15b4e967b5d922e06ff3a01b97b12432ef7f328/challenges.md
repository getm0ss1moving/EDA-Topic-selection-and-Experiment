## Independent analytic value screen (deduction, not measurement)

For a completed equal-prefix B3/M comparison, write B3 marginal cost as T=U+V, where U is exactly the repeated reachability work that valid cached cuts could avoid and V includes common candidate generation, joint reconstruction, physical oracles and output. Let actual avoided work be A<=U and added cache/construction/testing cost be K>=0. Then M costs T-A+K; saving is A-K. Positive saving requires A>K; even perfect free pruning cannot save more than U/T of marginal runtime. Whole-flow fraction is bounded more tightly by U/(F+T). This bound does not cover time-cap runs that inspect different candidates, and gives no numerical forecast without measurements.

Consequently collect reachability time avoided, cut creation/evaluation time and unchanged downstream work separately. A useful cut count alone does not establish speedup. This is an inexpensive accounting extension to existing T04/T05/T07, not a new method or experimental allocation. R05 §5.1 and R03 §7.2 reinforce why search overhead needs charging but their runtimes are not transferable bounds.
