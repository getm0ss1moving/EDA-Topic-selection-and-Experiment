## Complete-context, baseline-relative contract

CLM-B4-001, conditional elementary lemma, retained: a slot h identifies rule/version, anchor identity/geometry, layer/net semantics and query definition. A deterministic complete record q additionally contains all returned spatial-query contents, including an empty result, closed bounds, derived/global semantic prerequisites and every marker/source output required by the oracle. Hashes only index: canonical bytes must match. At most one record per slot per state is required, or a proved multiplicity bound must replace the unit-slot bound.

Let Q0 be all supported complete checkpoint records, Q_i those of sibling i, K0 the common initial certified cache, and R_i=Q_i minus K0. Complete warm means K0=Q0, with charged enumeration/certificate construction. An ordinary marker-only report is insufficient. Cold K0=empty and partial coverage are separate regimes; any unproven baseline slots must be added to the support, potentially making it universal.

Let E_i conservatively contain every slot whose complete record can change or appear relative to Q0. For a finite-radius supported predicate, include changed/new anchors and current anchors whose query intersects old **or** new edited geometry. Propagate net/component changes, derived-geometry changes, technology/rule/configuration, hierarchy transforms and worker boundary effects. Any unproved dependency gives all-slot support or UNKNOWN/full fallback. Old positive query memberships alone miss insertions into an empty range.

If q belongs to both R_i and R_j in a complete-warm regime, its slot lies in E_i intersection E_j. Otherwise it would be unchanged from Q0 and already in K0, a contradiction. Thus disjoint supports imply zero residual sharing. With nonnegative valid per-slot work bounds u_h, repeated predicate work is at most U_ij=sum u_h over their support intersection. This is a necessary screen and accounting bound, not a wall-time bound, physical measurement or new general theorem. It never certifies candidate cleanliness or permits discarding a candidate because sharing is absent.

## Acquisition mechanism and bounded planning

The anchored causal question remains whether paid acquisition of complete residual incidence, shared across competing candidate pairs, improves downstream decisions beyond equally informed conventional acquisition. The proposed F representation is a bipartite candidate-slot graph. Node inputs are checkpoint/candidate deltas, rule/layer/anchor geometry, baseline coverage and observed masks; hidden post-candidate exact payloads/results are unavailable until paid. A slot-conditioned encoder pools acquired contexts and supplies completion-value, cost and pair-saving estimates. Same-feature specialists plus fusion, a shared-trunk model and a direct-set predictor are fair alternatives; a shared encoder is not itself novelty.

An acquisition action materializes a declared slot group in eligible sibling states, returning canonical payload/output masks and actual CPU, bytes, elapsed time and state hashes. It updates every affected pair estimate. Exact reuse requires matching complete context; predictions schedule only. All arms use the same singleton/pair terminal kernel, deterministic ties, observations and fallback semantics. Under equal estimates, updates, costs, feasible actions and ties, they have identical choices (CLM-B4-003). Do not advertise C and F as distinct policies when implementations coincide.

The previous positive-one-slot-value stopping rule remains F1/C1, explicitly heuristic. F2/C2 may additionally choose an unordered two-slot bundle. Score each eligible singleton/bundle as estimated expected best terminal value after observing it, minus acquisition charge; compare with STOP. This is depth-two open-loop bundle evaluation, not optimal adaptive planning or a guarantee against all complementary information. Both C and F get the same bundles and scenario budget. A small depth-three diagnostic is allowed only for an already bounded tiny pool and receives equal accounting; it is not a deployable advantage for F alone.

Research-specification caps, to be embodied in an immutable future configuration: at most 16 siblings, 4096 support-synopsis slots per pool, 8 active query slots selected deterministically from deployable bounds/costs/slot IDs, 64 joint model scenarios, 8 acquisition decision steps, 36 singleton/two-slot actions and 136 terminal singleton/pair subsets. Creating the synopsis/shortlist costs time and memory. Overflow or incomplete enumeration disables sharing inference and returns ordinary checking/UNKNOWN; it does not discard a physical candidate. Terminal scoring under these caps has at most 313344 scenario/subset evaluations per step, 2506752 over eight steps in the simple exhaustive implementation, excluding graph/encoder/serialization/checker work. This is an operation-count upper bound, not measured time or allocated compute. No training architecture or physical label budget is ready; EXP-104 fitting remains closed until an exact training manifest and allocation are agreed.

Predicted acquisition utility may be wrong. A wall deadline reserves complete final-oracle/connectivity/extraction/timing work; use a conservative allocated reserve or return no verified completion if it cannot finish. Every selected candidate must complete all supported exact checks and unsupported reference work, marker regeneration/removal and connectivity guards. The final chosen state pays the complete named reference. Partial/censored work earns no verified-success credit. Do not claim checking savings when an unconditional repeated full pass dominates them.
