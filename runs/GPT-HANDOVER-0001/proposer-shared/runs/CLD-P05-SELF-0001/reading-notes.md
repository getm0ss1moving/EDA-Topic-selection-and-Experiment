## Proposer assessment (inference; needs the verifier)

1. **K1 is not triggered by SRC-012, SRC-066, SRC-067 or SRC-068**, at the reading quality stated above. The three parameterized works certify over a continuous parameter box under an affine (SRC-066, SRC-067) or class-F (SRC-068) delay model. They do not use the discrete characterized scenarios that K1 names. SRC-012 uses discrete corners but offers no guarantee.
2. **CLM-026 needs two corrections.**
   - SRC-068 is not an elimination method. It contributes guaranteed max-operator bounds, so it belongs with the bounding precedents, not with path elimination.
   - Certified pruning followed by exact evaluation is a known structure, not only certified elimination. SRC-067 (Sec. 4.2) and US 8,245,167 B1 discard fan-in cones whose bound cannot beat the incumbent, then evaluate the rest exactly. EDA-0002's bound-certify-refine loop has the same shape.
   - The remaining distinction is therefore:
     - bounds per scenario from that scenario's own characterized tables and parasitics, with no cross-scenario parametric model;
     - soundness with respect to a reference engine's delay calculation, including slew, setup/hold and exceptions (P8, P9);
     - the objective: reproducing all decisions below the margin M at every scenario, rather than finding one worst corner or path.
   - This narrows CLM-015 further and should be stated plainly in the response.
3. **Commercial evidence.** US 8,245,167 B1 shows that a Cadence-assigned method for branch-and-bound critical-timing search with bound-based path pruning was filed in 2008 and covered setup and hold. It belongs in `industry-gap.md` beside SRC-017 and SRC-018 as described, not shipped, capability.
4. **Residual risk.** The TCAD 2010 paper is the closest unread item, because it claims exact worst-slack for sequential circuits. It should be added to K1's list with a verifier read requested. The NTU thesis is a second, lower-priority item, since SRC-014 already covers its content.
5. **Stopping rule (`docs/EVIDENCE.md`).** Two documented expansions are now complete: the backward-citation pass over SRC-014 (round 1b) and this forward-citation pass. Neither revealed a closer mechanism with discrete characterized scenarios. The stopping rule would be met except that TCAD 2010 is unread, so it is reported as residual risk.
