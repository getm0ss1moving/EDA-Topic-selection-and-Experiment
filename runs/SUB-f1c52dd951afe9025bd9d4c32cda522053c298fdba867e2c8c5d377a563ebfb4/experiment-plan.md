# Conditional experiment plan, revision 1

This is a complete reviewable research plan, not executable authorization. EXP-000 precedes all future EDA builds, baseline runs, incidence labels, training and full flow. The batch grants source reading and tiny own analytical checks only. No physical adapter/model exists in this release.

## Target, oracle and frozen decisions

H1 is proposal.md CLM-009/018; CLM-010 useful incidence is a prerequisite. Use a legal pre-CTS ideal-clock single-corner state, equivalent single-height master substitutions and a distinct-cell absolute-site relocation. Fix legalization displacement limits, instance orientation/fixed/blockage rules, process failure/censoring semantics, global returned-diff logging and exact restore hashes. Whole-design GR/RC/OpenSTA supplies ranking feedback. Equivalence, legality, electrical constraints and routing checks are separate. A fixed identical CTS-if-required/DR/extraction/timing suffix evaluates incumbents, including KEEP, under the same routing allowance. No final-route outcome is an initial feature.

Primary endpoint: complete charged time to a prespecified post-DR feasible-quality target, with failures/right-censoring at the cap. Secondary endpoint: best feasible effective period versus complete CPU-seconds and elapsed time. Report all constraint failures, TNS, area, estimated power, overflow, DRC, memory and restore cost. Thresholds and allowed regressions await owner validation in the manifest, before execution consent. Calibration of repeat noise is a prespecified future stage and cannot retroactively set a favorable improvement threshold.

All learned arms share candidate pool, success/gate/fallback code, label availability, auxiliary singleton supervision and feature acquisition. Main representation comparison uses identical complete observed records. Report a separate availability expansion; never attribute its extra samples to representation. Train/predict loss in reconstructed normalized metric space; use training-only per-metric scales common to both orders, no independent contrast normalization. Match hyperparameter trials, early stopping, parameter/tuning budgets and all optimizer effects or state the identified difference. Compare B5 to B-CD to isolate singleton factorization, to symmetry-matched B3 to isolate sharing/parameterization, and to B3-T to establish algebraic equivalence. Do not require super-additivity, or claim unexplained gain is a new mechanism.

Faithful HyPAS/AgenticPD/R09 original references are added only if scope and resources allow. Restricted adaptations are labelled adaptations; missing original methods remain novelty limitations. Post-CTS ResizerAgent execution requires an unavailable provider subscription and a different stage; it is a source comparator, not silently run or represented as an equal original baseline.

## Data, allocation and limits

No dataset acquired. Future rights-audited candidate family pool: lawful ORFS/public contest designs, pinned by full source/tool/config hashes and license notices. Split by underlying design/RTL family before checkpoint generation; variants, constraints and seeds remain together. Initial proposed feasibility split: six families, 3 train/1 validation/2 test, two checkpoints/family. This is not a representative confirmatory sample. LLM/public-benchmark pretraining overlap unknown; no model/API calls are allocated.

K<=4+4 creates <=16 cross pairs. Per checkpoint upper bound: 1 KEEP +8 singleton+32 ordered final states=41 GR/STA observations; prefixes recomputed: 8+64=72 primitive applications. Across twelve checkpoints: <=492 states and <=864 primitives, excluding repeats, failed/censored attempts, restore, implementation preflight and final suffix. These are arithmetic requests, not resource estimates. Gate must cap every category before running. Incidence phase begins with <=2 training checkpoints, K=2+2: <=13 states/checkpoint before prespecified repeats. No held-out favorable-case selection. Stop if repeat noise overwhelms contrast, masks/restore fail, or expected decision value cannot plausibly pay for overhead.

Proposed optional model cap: CPU only, GBDT and <=100k-parameter MLP, three seeds and <=12 validation configurations per architecture inclusive of gate tuning; actual time/RAM allocation is unknown. No paid/GPU/remote fallback. Data generation, training, validation search, failed moves, model/agent inference, feature acquisition, caching, communication and final-route suffix all enter cold-start and amortized budgets. Common caching is charged equally.

## Decisive tests and stopping

1. Contract coverage includes all nine syntactic masks, partial UNKNOWNs, report availability and rescue; physical reachable subset must be justified.
2. Repeat-run noise, disjoint-support negative controls, crowded-row cases and full returned geometry determine whether real order effects exist.
3. Learning curves, zero-d, shuffled-d, residual-versus-no-residual, separate-versus-shared metric heads and symmetry controls attribute any gain. Future realized geometry is diagnostic/label only.
4. All-pair policy performance includes fallback/gate errors, skipped rescued branches and censoring. Complete-only RMSE cannot pass.
5. B3-T and B7 must match their equivalents; mismatch blocks interpretation.
6. Held-out final-DR reversals or strong baseline domination kill the utility claim; no larger model or favorable family search to conceal failure.

Analyze paired family-level effects with intervals and all failures; two test families cannot support population significance. Later confirmatory allocation and sample size require pilot variance, fixed engineering targets, fresh plan and matching exact consents. EXP-009 is post-outcome review only, never retroactive execution approval. New task/dependency bytes invalidate consent.
