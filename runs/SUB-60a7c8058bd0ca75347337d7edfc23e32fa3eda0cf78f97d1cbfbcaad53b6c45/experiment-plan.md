## CT04: exact cost definitions and symbolic ledger

Define F as original fixed design flow from the declared deployment entry point through saved incumbent, original full extraction and all-endpoint timing; T04 pins this entry point. O0 is B0's extra matched no-op checkpoint load/round-trip/full validation. For repair arm a, R_a is its entire marginal stage from loading that same checkpoint through final accepted or restored output, including all failed/rejected work and all repeated oracle calls. B0 has R_0=O0.

Deployment whole-flow cost C_a=F+R_a; C_0=F+O0. The provisional **20% decision denominator is C_0**, so (C_M-C_0)/C_0 <=0.20, computed per paired case. Also report marginal added cost R_a-O0 in absolute CPU/wall seconds and R_a/O0 (undefined if O0=0; never replace by zero). Strongest-baseline repair comparisons use C_M/C_b and R_M/R_b separately. A large F is not evidence that marginal repair is acceptable: practitioner must validate an absolute repair-cost cap at T02/T03 before execution. No such cap is invented here.

Mutually exclusive leaf timer categories: SETUP_BUILD, SETUP_DATA, SETUP_DEVELOPMENT; INCUMBENT_FLOW; CHECKPOINT_IO; SELECTOR_PREPROCESS; SELECTOR_SEARCH; JOINT_RECONSTRUCTION; DRC_CONNECTIVITY; EXTRACT_STA; POWER_GUARD; ROLLBACK; FINAL_OUTPUT; ANALYSIS. Each timed interval is one leaf, with trial/arm/candidate/phase IDs, monotonic wall start/end, process-tree user+system CPU deltas, bytes, peak RSS and success/reason. No parent plus child duration may both be charged. Parallel process CPU sums, wall is envelope/critical path; concurrent processes need exclusive attribution. Failed calls keep their categories. Resource cap wall and CPU timers cover every marginal phase, not only search.

Worked symbolic example (not measured): F=f_load+f_synth+f_place+f_clock+f_route+f_extract+f_sta. O0=o_io+o_check+o_extract+o_sta. Two failed candidates and a success give R_M=m_io+m_pre+m_select+sum over j=1..3 (m_joint_j+m_check_j+m_extract_j+m_sta_j+m_power_j)+sum over rejected j=1..2 m_rollback_j+m_final. Every symbol occurs once. Then C_M/C_0=(F+R_M)/(F+O0), while added marginal cost is R_M-O0. Zero-time skipped phases are explicitly logged, not silently omitted. If a final oracle is reused after exact unchanged checkpoint identity, log a cache hit and its validation overhead; no hypothetical oracle duration is charged.

Scientific batch budget differs from deployment accounting: construct F once per unique checkpoint and charge it once in total actual CPU; charge each real R_a once, plus setup/development/build/analysis. Deployment tables attribute the same measured F once to each counterfactual arm without claiming it was recomputed six times. No oracle result can be reused across different route-geometry hashes. Shared original checkpoint reuse and immutable setup must be symmetric; method-specific preprocessing goes in its R_a. Build/tuning costs are separately visible and included in total allocation, not multiplied per deployed chip or hidden in F.
