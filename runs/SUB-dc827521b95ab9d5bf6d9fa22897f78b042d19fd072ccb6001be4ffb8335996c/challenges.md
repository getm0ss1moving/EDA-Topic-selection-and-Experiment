## OBJ-004 — Candidate cap makes three-owner coverage impossible at the declared maximum pool

Target: R00 proposal mechanism step 4; experiment-plan Shared selector contract/B3/M; CLM-010 and T04/T05. Severity: moderate, fixable specification/interpretability concern; not a refutation of all cut speedups. Evidence is the frozen algorithm specification and the following elementary derivation, not a physical measurement.

For n=32, cardinality-first enumeration has 1+32+C(32,2)=529 subsets before any triple. A 256-first-seen-subset cap therefore visits at most 223 pairs after empty/singletons and no triple. Even if empty is excluded, there are 528 nonempty subsets before triples. Since cut-pruned subsets count, cut pruning cannot extend this prefix. More generally n>=23 excludes every triple under this rule (1+23+253=277). Construct a target graph whose only admissible corridor contains three disjoint required owner labels and add enough irrelevant owners to reach 32: no size<=2 release is feasible, but the three-owner release is. M and cardinality-ordered B3 stop before it. This is a specification counterexample, not executed data.

Applicability: exact for the written global cardinality-first first-seen counting policy. If common superset completion interleaves triples ahead of remaining pairs, it contradicts or qualifies that order and the exact merge schedule must be specified. B4 can otherwise get a materially different prefix through seed completion; this is a policy distinction to report, not inherently unfair.

Consequence: budgeted failure cannot test the entire claimed <=3-owner neighborhood, and cut time savings cannot increase coverage when the subset cap is the binding limit. It can still save time/reachability states or improve time-to-same-result; no theorem rules that out. Immutable owner IDs can also bias which late pairs are reached.

Confidence: high in arithmetic, unresolved in physical prevalence and ultimate implementation. Reversal condition: pin an unambiguous shared schedule with declared cardinality coverage, or explicitly constrain/restate tested neighborhoods and retain excluded cases in the denominator. Do not silently waive the cap for M. Cheapest test: documentary candidate-rank table for n=3,22,23,32 and a declared three-label corridor; once authorized, trace exact considered/pruned/reconstructed subsets, ID permutations and binding caps. Report a time-cap-binding stratum separately from subset-cap-binding runs. Pass when the claimed coverage equals actual traces and useful supersets remain reachable under the documented schedule.
