## SC-R3: exact candidate and reconstruction contract

This replaces the ambiguous R2 statement that all arms simultaneously share cardinality order and native-seed superset completion. The owner universe is the same; the prefixes need not be. No cap increase, private M waiver, altered downstream endpoint or new experiment is introduced.

Common domain: n<=32 signal owners, <=3 blockers plus the target, same fixed directed cost-expanded H (<=128 consumed-cost states per routing vertex), all-owner labels, immutable IDs, clips, path generator and compatibility rules. q(e)=ceil(w(e)/delta), Q=floor(B/delta); physical weights/B/delta/clip/path limits still require the T04 development specification. Zero arcs use visited-state deduplication. Unsupported rules fail closed; failure in H is not physical infeasibility. Released owners may retain original routes. The target path generator for every arm is restricted to the identical H and target budget Q; otherwise graph-infeasible pruning could discard a physically considered alternative and CLM-019 would not apply. Owners are reembedded using the shared finite path domain, whose physical pinning is still T04. Report this common truncation as a domain limit, never global physical infeasibility.

A complete candidate is a distinct owner subset delivered to that arm's physical-candidate inspector. Its first delivery consumes one of 256 slots before any cut/prune/reconstruction decision, including empty; duplicates consume no new subset slot but all duplicate-handling time is charged. Label-state search nodes, including sink labels used as internal seeds, count toward states/work and do not consume a candidate slot until physically submitted. Log both seeds and submissions, preventing hidden search. All popped/expanded selection/reachability/reconstruction states count toward 50,000 total states with named categories; work in clauses, bitsets, dominance, duplicate generation, setup and oracles is timed. CPU, wall, RSS and correctness caps can stop sooner; their actual physical limits/allocation are T03/T04 prerequisites. At cap, record uninspected pending candidates and reason; never claim exhaustive coverage.

**B3/M stream:** generate G_n in increasing cardinality 0,1,2,3, then lexicographic tuple of immutable owner IDs. Inspect each first-seen subset; M tests cached valid cuts before reachability, B3 does not. A cut rejection consumes its subset slot and supplies no free replacement. No seed-priority interleaving is used by B3 or M. Completeness means only the admitted prefix, not the entire <=3 universe. proposal.md CT06 pins the ranks/counts at n=3,22,23,32. At n>=23 no triple is admitted; at n=22 only two are.

**B4 native and completion merge:** adaptation of S20/S21's characteristic label-set search, not published EDA code. Native state is (expanded vertex x,owner set L), initially (s,empty); expand directed adjacency with union L'=L union L(e), discard |L'|>3, retain inclusion-incomparable labels at the same expanded vertex. Use heap key (|L|,owner tuple,expanded vertex ID), deterministic sorted adjacency and a visited/inclusion table. Only identical expanded vertices use inclusion dominance; no extra cross-cost dominance is used. At the sink, each newly popped label seed A is valid target information even if later dominated; record it and do not erase earlier physical candidates. Continue after the first seed.

For any seed A, the common completion function Sup(A) is every S with A subseteq S, |S|<=3, sorted by (|S|,owner tuple), including A. Its lazy iterator advances once per merge turn for each discovered seed, with seeds traversed in discovery order; generated distinct sets enter a min-heap keyed (|S|,owner tuple). A merge turn performs exactly one native state pop/expansion if available, advances each active seed iterator by one, then submits at most one smallest pending unseen set. If none is pending, repeat turns. Remove exhausted iterators, keep expanding after failed reembedding, and continue until native/iterators/pending heap all exhaust or a declared common cap stops the arm. Duplicate heap entries are suppressed but timed. If a seed has a dominated label it cannot justify deleting its physical supersets. B4's early triple may precede an as-yet undiscovered lower-cardinality seed; this is an explicit policy difference and is logged. There is no claimed common B3/M rank for B4. Under no cap and finite graph, this stream reaches every target-feasible physical subset: every feasible set contains a sink seed, and Sup supplies it. Under the cap there is no such guarantee. Native expansions and iterator work are charged; B4 receives neither free preprocessing nor a weakened scalar label-count oracle.

This merge is a deterministic harness choice requiring independent review, not evidence that B4 is the best possible implementation. T04 must expose native and candidate traces and retain an uncapped tiny exact reference. If a more source-faithful or stronger bounded B4 is identified, revise and re-consent before execution; no claimed win may depend on suppressing it. B2's policy remains source-mapped at T04, not silently assigned B3 order.

**Reconstruction and commit:** all arms use the identical deterministic bounded route-assignment enumerator over a subset, not a special seed path. Assignment/path limits remain unimplemented T04 dependencies; no physical executability claim. Every trial starts from the same immutable original checkpoint, including after a previous candidate passes. Save all passers; after the arm stops, return maximum all-endpoint WNS, tie by lexicographic geometry digest, or original if no passing change. Path/candidate truncation is censored. Cache clauses only under the full CT08 context key; invalidate on context/geometry changes. Never derive target cuts from physical/STA failure without a separate valid frozen-H proof. Exact tiny oracle means complete declared finite graph/path domain; a capped run is never exact.

## CT04: exact cost definitions and symbolic ledger

Define F as original fixed design flow from the declared deployment entry point through saved incumbent, original full extraction and all-endpoint timing; T04 pins this entry point. O0 is B0's extra matched no-op checkpoint load/round-trip/full validation. For repair arm a, R_a is its entire marginal stage from loading that same checkpoint through final accepted or restored output, including all failed/rejected work and all repeated oracle calls. B0 has R_0=O0.

Deployment whole-flow cost C_a=F+R_a; C_0=F+O0. The provisional **20% decision denominator is C_0**, so (C_M-C_0)/C_0 <=0.20, computed per paired case. Also report marginal added cost R_a-O0 in absolute CPU/wall seconds and R_a/O0 (undefined if O0=0; never replace by zero). Strongest-baseline repair comparisons use C_M/C_b and R_M/R_b separately. A large F is not evidence that marginal repair is acceptable: practitioner must validate an absolute repair-cost cap at T02/T03 before execution. No such cap is invented here.

Mutually exclusive leaf timer categories: SETUP_BUILD, SETUP_DATA, SETUP_DEVELOPMENT; INCUMBENT_FLOW; CHECKPOINT_IO; SELECTOR_PREPROCESS; SELECTOR_SEARCH; JOINT_RECONSTRUCTION; DRC_CONNECTIVITY; EXTRACT_STA; POWER_GUARD; ROLLBACK; FINAL_OUTPUT; ANALYSIS. Each timed interval is one leaf, with trial/arm/candidate/phase IDs, monotonic wall start/end, process-tree user+system CPU deltas, bytes, peak RSS and success/reason. No parent plus child duration may both be charged. Parallel process CPU sums, wall is envelope/critical path; concurrent processes need exclusive attribution. Failed calls keep their categories. Resource cap wall and CPU timers cover every marginal phase, not only search.

Worked symbolic example (not measured): F=f_load+f_synth+f_place+f_clock+f_route+f_extract+f_sta. O0=o_io+o_check+o_extract+o_sta. Two failed candidates and a success give R_M=m_io+m_pre+m_select+sum over j=1..3 (m_joint_j+m_check_j+m_extract_j+m_sta_j+m_power_j)+sum over rejected j=1..2 m_rollback_j+m_final. Every symbol occurs once. Then C_M/C_0=(F+R_M)/(F+O0), while added marginal cost is R_M-O0. Zero-time skipped phases are explicitly logged, not silently omitted. If a final oracle is reused after exact unchanged checkpoint identity, log a cache hit and its validation overhead; no hypothetical oracle duration is charged.

Scientific batch budget differs from deployment accounting: construct F once per unique checkpoint and charge it once in total actual CPU; charge each real R_a once, plus setup/development/build/analysis. Deployment tables attribute the same measured F once to each counterfactual arm without claiming it was recomputed six times. No oracle result can be reused across different route-geometry hashes. Shared original checkpoint reuse and immutable setup must be symmetric; method-specific preprocessing goes in its R_a. Build/tuning costs are separately visible and included in total allocation, not multiplied per deployed chip or hidden in F.
