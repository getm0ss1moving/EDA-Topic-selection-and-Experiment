Target CLM-403/405/406/407/408; major unresolved advancement gate, not refutation. Sources C01 pp.666-667 EQ2/3; C02 EQ11-18; P0 W1 and T01. Facts: W1 only separates a coarse bound; scalar endpoint elimination can be strictly stronger as above. Conditional cost null: when both coordinates are evaluated at every visited paired state and no relational enclosure stops early, scalar memoization can reuse each distinct projected (node,endpoint,context,slew) state; pairs may repeat projections. This is a comparator obligation, not a general lower-bound theorem—relational bounds may genuinely avoid scalar work.

Confidence high on missing witness and scalar inequality; unknown on eventual net benefit. Reversal: exhibit a lawful enclosure with a fully charged finite cost ledger in which paired inference stops before equivalent scalar information is required, with the same available transfer information, cache budget and preprocessing. Cheapest decisive test: paper-level T01 ledger including the adverse two-suffix example, a favorable case, and a projection-reuse comparator. Stop the proposed mechanism if only disadvantaged scalar controls produce the advantage. Do not infer universal saturation from a failed witness.

### OBJ-402 — Executable exactness and amortization are jointly unproved

Target CLM-404/410 and T04/T06; major readiness gap explicitly acknowledged by P. C03 Table2::findValue has float inputs/return and double interpolation intermediates. The mathematical difference recurrence does not itself bound the actual operation sequence on absolute arrival states. P0's full-state M repair is conceptually sound, but changes memoization keys and potentially destroys reuse.

Inference: soundness and claimed cost advantage must be proved on the same implementation, rather than proving a full-state method and timing a simplified slew-pair cache. Confidence high in the missing artifact, not a claim that such an enclosure is impossible. Reversal: source-locked executable enclosure proof, operation-order specification, independent enumerator, and same-ledger full-state/fast-summary reconciliation. Cheapest test before build: symbolic rounding budget at one interpolated gate plus setup, varying absolute arrivals with the same slew pair; specify directed enclosure and UNKNOWN cases. Later authorized boundary tests must include compiler contraction/FMA, overflow/nonfinite inputs and table extrapolation. A diagnostic 5ps tolerance never validates an incorrect exact deletion.
