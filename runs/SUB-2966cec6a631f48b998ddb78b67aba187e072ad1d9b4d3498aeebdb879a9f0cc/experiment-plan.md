## Endpoint-matched stopping and information value

First-clean endpoint needs a complete accepted result and sound disqualifications; full ranked nonclean fallback needs complete reports for every ranked candidate. Preregister which outcome is primary and when fallback is requested. Continue after rejection is an acquisition action, not mandatory wasted work: its extra observations may help another sibling but must pay predicate, key, allocation, cancellation and opportunity costs. With pure complete keys and a non-evicting cache, completing all siblings has the same union predicate work under any order. Moving a shared query from the next sibling to a rejected sibling does not reduce that union; setup, information timing or changed candidate selection can alter total cost. The own toy shows both continuation cost signs, not physical benefit.

C202 uses full-report and witness-stop/continue crossed with C/F, same fixed order for isolating stopping, then same allowed adaptive planning for main comparison. Controls: all clean; early and late valid witness; no clean; provisional marker removed by later semantics; missing negative dependency; shared-work-rich invalid sibling; forced cache eviction; interrupted-worker restart; no safe witness subset; unsupported full fallback. Measure cache entries gained/lost, bytes, predicate/key work, restart time, full returned report and all censored cases. Without a safe interface retain full checks and explicitly report stopping inapplicability; do not invent a physical saving.
