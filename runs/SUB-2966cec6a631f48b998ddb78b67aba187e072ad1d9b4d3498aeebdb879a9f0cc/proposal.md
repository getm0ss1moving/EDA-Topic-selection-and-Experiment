## Complete-context and paid common-cache contract

CLM-B4-001 is retained as an elementary conditional derivation. A slot h includes rule/version, anchor identity/geometry, layer/net semantics and query definition. Its **input key** contains complete deterministic semantic prerequisites, full canonical query contents including empty results, closed bounds, derived/global context and invocation flags. Its **cached value** contains predicate result and canonical marker/source outputs. Outputs are not secretly part of a cheap key. Materializing an input may cost more than the predicate; both are charged separately. Hashes index records; reuse also requires byte equality. One complete record per slot/state, or an explicit validated multiplicity bound, is required.

Q0 is the complete supported checkpoint record set; Q_i is sibling i's set. Every arm receives identical paid K0. Complete warm means K0=Q0 with charged coverage/certificate construction; marker-only reports are insufficient. Cold and partial regimes include uncovered baseline obligations in their support or use UNKNOWN/full fallback. R_i=Q_i minus K0. Conservative E_i contains every slot that may change or appear: old and new edit halos, anchor creation/deletion, range insertions/deletions including previously empty queries, polygon/component closure, hierarchy transforms, technology/config/rule versions, net relabeling and worker-boundary dependencies. Unproved closure is universal support or fallback, never optimistic reuse.

If q belongs to R_i intersection R_j with complete K0, its slot must lie in E_i intersection E_j: otherwise q is unchanged from Q0 and already cached. For valid nonnegative per-slot predicate-work bounds u_h, repeated predicate work is at most the sum of u_h over that intersection. This bounds repeated predicate work only; it is neither a wall-time guarantee nor clean certification nor new general mathematics. Disjoint supports do not justify discarding a physical candidate. Official ISPD2019 FAQ12 is an attributed real negative-window dependency example, not an adapter proof.
