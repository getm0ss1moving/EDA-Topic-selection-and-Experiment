# Industry gap and demand limits

Engineering user: ASIC physical-design/clock implementation engineer choosing leaf groups and buffer locations at a fixed placed checkpoint. The harmful event of interest is shared scarce legal footprint competition that forces relocation or regrouping and affects final route/timing. Broad displacement-to-QoR relevance is documented by pinned OpenROAD S01; the narrow competition event's frequency and value remain a hypothesis requiring EXP-1202.

Pinned OpenROAD selected functions S02/S03 already perform clustering, obstacle-aware movement and occupancy checks. This dossier does not report an OpenROAD bug or claim a lack of joint reasoning throughout its entire CTS/placement flow. iCTS S04 and ML-clustering patent S05 further narrow any method gap. A gap in an open interface alone would be engineering work, not a scientific contribution.

Cadence's public CCOpt page S10 advertises merging clock synthesis with timing optimization/useful skew. That is advertised capability only. Closed internals, full legal-site conflict handling, deployment prevalence and matched performance are unknown. No commercial license or competitive benchmark was obtained. Patent disclosures S05/S06 are prior mechanisms, not product implementation/performance evidence or a legal opinion.

The candidate needs a useful representation/amortization delta over equally informed integrated solver and ordinary decomposition. The exact-cover formulation, no-good cut, classifier plus checker, multiple workers, and conventional legalizer are not individually new claims. Narrow deployment value may be absent if ordinary joint assignment or downstream cell movement cheaply resolves the issue; EXP-1202/1204 must settle that before expensive learning.

Adoption costs: freeze placed database, enumerate legal rows/footprints, reconcile OpenDB/CTS coordinates and orientation/padding, instrument full flow, expose bounded regrouping, maintain domain/cut versioning, and collect offline labels with rights. Clock legality does not imply route/timing closure. Benefits must survive final setup/hold, route DRC/connectivity, area/power and total latency. Threshold and engineer validation remain unresolved. Conditional domain restriction to fixed standard cells is explicitly compared against both adapted and native stock flows; it cannot silently become an artificially difficult industry problem.
