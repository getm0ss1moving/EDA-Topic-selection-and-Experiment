# Sharper challenger review and automatic portfolio retirement

The owner requested stronger criticism of topic rationale/innovation and a greater willingness to stop weak topics. The challenger now leads with consequential attacks, compares exact prior/conventional mechanisms, tests real engineer decisions, searches for minimal counterexamples, audits complete costs and offers falsifiers plus anchor-preserving repairs. It does not manufacture objection counts or reject useful combinations by category.

The independent scientific verifier grades seven bound axes. Novelty, utility and mechanism receive weight 2, as does anchor viability; headroom, contest originality and experiments receive weight 1. Grades clear/unresolved/major/fatal contribute 0/1/2/3. One valid, source-checked, anchor-unrepairable fatal axis retires immediately. Score >=6 on two consecutive distinct scientific revisions with at least two shared nonclear axes also retires, as a portfolio decision rather than a refutation of unresolved premises. These concrete defaults are implementation choices under the owner instruction.

The new runtime decision is retired_by_policy, separate from signed joint abandonment. No proposer consent or scientific approval is fabricated. Exact frozen outputs, checked policy, original scientific action, scorecard and resurrection conditions remain in the affected round PR, including retirement.json. Successors receive fresh IDs/seats and released negative evidence after the required audit; existing finite domain limits still stop work. Old frozen runs are never rescored.

Score history is reconstructed from immutable scientific releases, and policy bytes must match their state/packet binding. Refuted/unsupported/nonblocking attacks cannot drive retirement; one claim cannot inflate multiple axes. Headroom-fatal requires a bound below a locked numeric meaningful-effect threshold. Temporary access or budget gaps are not fatal evidence.
