## Evidence-backed objections

### OBJ-001 — Closest residual-function baseline is absent from the actual arm definition

Target: CLM-010; “Closest work and changed structure”; experiment-plan B2 and T01/T06. Severity: major, fixable research-design concern; high confidence in overlap, unresolved useful specialization.

Evidence: [C01](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1401.3448), Definitions13/22/23 and section5.2/Proposition1, merges equivalent subproblems and implements reductions with hash tables. [C02](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2024/0219.pdf), section3 pp1981–1982, groups prefixes by identical valid suffix sets and retains the best representative. These mechanisms are closer to the proposal than uncompressed assignment-state elimination.

Reasoning (independent reduction): write one variable x_i per pin and binary compatibility factors f_ij. After fixing a valid prefix, the remaining relation is the unchanged suffix factors conjoined with unary exclusions x_j notin B_j. Thus sigma is a concrete residual-CSP key, and its labelled DAG is an ordered decision representation. No geometric information enters this rule beyond the original conflict graph. B2 currently specifies ordinary assignment states, so a win over B2 cannot isolate novelty beyond established semantic merging. The proposer candidly acknowledges this risk; this objection supplies a closer mapping rather than contradicting a claimed established invention.

Reversal: identify a precise additional structural property/capability and its advantage over a faithful reduced-diagram comparator on the same mask semantics. Cheapest test: a paper-level operation-by-operation reduction and comparator specification before implementation. Replace or supplement B2 with reduced MDD/hash-consed residual compilation, preserve all mask-dependent route labels, and charge compilation and order selection. No mandatory sixth full physical arm or extra budget is implied; re-plan first.

Nonblocking precision: equal sigma is sufficient, not necessary for semantic continuation equivalence. Example: A={a0,a1}, B={b0,b1}, C={c}; conflicts (a0,b0),(b0,c). Prefix a0 forbids b0 whereas a1 does not, but both have the sole suffix (b1,c). The scheme keeps distinct signatures although suffix sets agree. This is not unsoundness or evidence of slower actual execution; it shows that maximal quotient compression is unproved. The current proof only claims the sufficient direction and is sound within its declared model.

### OBJ-002 — “All graphs” and the bounded test budget are inconsistent unless explicitly restricted

Target: experiment-plan Correctness item1 versus tasklist T03 and 0.25 CPU-hour request. Severity: major executable-plan ambiguity; high confidence, easily repaired.

Evidence: G01 states “all graphs up to the predeclared bounded size”; T03 specifies <=6 pins, <=3 routes/pin and a documented generator. Derivation: six pins with three routes admit binomial(6,2)*3*3=135 possible inter-pin conflict edges, hence 2^135=43556142965880123323311949751266331066368 labelled graphs, before masks. Enumerating assignments is only 3^6 per fixed graph; those quantifiers are different. This is elementary combinatorics, not an experimental timing prediction.

Reversal: explicitly say all assignments for each member of a finite enumerated graph suite, and list a much smaller exhaustive graph tier with exact counts. Cheapest test: inspect the generator specification and cardinalities. Require deterministic adversarial fixtures, a fixed random tier, seed/hash/count locks and a hard cost limit; do not advertise exhaustive arbitrary-graph coverage. No criticism of the soundness proof follows from finite testing alone.

### OBJ-003 — Native-policy equivalence remains a prerequisite, not a completed solver replacement

Target: CLM-007/009/010; T04–T07. Severity: major unresolved integration/value gate, already acknowledged; high confidence in dependency, unknown impact.

Evidence: [C04](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cuhk-eda/dr-cu-fastpass/cd66e1091ea31f419161918e56d546a297644f40/src/pa/PAHelper.cpp), solveInstanceSAT/buildAssumption and solver.conflict loop, chooses relaxations from the SAT engine's conflict. Trivial components also bypass solving. [C03](https://wfz0755.github.io/CV/archive/2023-ISPD-Fastpass.pdf), sections3.4.1/4.2, describes decomposition and small solving workloads. [C05](https://wfz0755.github.io/CV/archive/wfz-thesis.pdf), section4.2.5 pp76–78, documents min-area/history-cost integration changes; p88 records a guide-dependent exception. These are published/source observations, not local measurements.

Reasoning: SAT/UNSAT alone does not reproduce a native conflict-core-selected sequence. Replaying a captured sequence is a controlled diagnostic, not a drop-in native-flow speedup. Canonical geometry is enforced only within controlled arms; native N can differ. No concrete solver-independent core/tie policy or allocation is provided, so CLM-009's identical-work bound cannot automatically be applied to N. The proposal correctly marks this stop condition; retain it.

Reversal: source-pinned implementable outer-policy contract, complete charged native/control traces, unchanged relevant geometry and a measured useful opportunity fraction after allocation. Cheapest present action is source-level control-flow specification, not running an unallocated pilot. Future test: report native skip rates, actual queries, preparation/solve/downstream exclusive costs, core-policy and output equality; changed output needs separate full-flow attribution. Do not derive a numerical saving from published plots or repeated fabricated queries.

## Independent headroom and positive findings

If actual query count q and mean savings per query d were constant, amortization requires K<q*d; that simplified condition must include canonicalization/fallback and heterogeneous queries in real accounting. No numeric confidence interval is defensible. State explosion, overhead-dominated ties or useful special-case speedup all remain possible.
