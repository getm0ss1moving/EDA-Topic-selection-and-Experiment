## Strong, naive-composition, and equal-budget baselines

For a combination, test its claimed system-level delta, not merely each component's age. Where relevant, compare specialists plus ordinary fusion, a shared unified baseline, selective learned/exact hybrids, strong conventional methods, and single-agent repeated calls under matched data/tools and complete resource accounting. Explain which comparisons apply; do not require every architecture for every topic. Charge training, labels, model calls, EDA/checker calls, failed trials, communication, cache construction and fallback. Super-additivity is not mandatory for a useful systems contribution.
