ideas verbatim. Record commit/hash/session provenance; export only common policy,

job. No conversation forks, private notes, shared memory or scientific messages.

releases, negative evidence and distinct topic IDs. Apply verified retirement
certificates without pretending unresolved topics are disproved. Scientific
confirmation still needs complete evidence, exact human/challenger task-list
agreement and allocated resources. A configured seat is not a running service.

checks, then merge transport/governance without self-approval. Keep credentials
outside role packets. The shared OS account provides policy isolation only.
