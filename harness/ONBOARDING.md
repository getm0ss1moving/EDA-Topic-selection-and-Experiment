# Fresh role onboarding

Active roles: searcher, challenger, verifier. Read common policy and only your
assigned harness from the frozen packet. Verify input hashes; record actor,
session, role, commit, permitted outputs, source access and any extra context.
Use own scratch/outbox; no other sessions, harnesses, private notes, memories or
messages. Do not claim OS isolation on a shared host.

Searcher receives a bounded literature batch. Challenger waits for a human idea;
verifier waits for its matching idea/challenge pair. Missing inputs mean waiting,
not approval or a self-generated topic. No subordinate agents or experiments are
authorized by onboarding. Freeze own outputs; coordinator transports them by PR.
